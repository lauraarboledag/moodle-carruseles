Introduction

Why is your country important?

Your country of origin is part of your cultural identity. It's one of the first things we share when we meet someone.

It connects us with our history and traditions!

When do you mention your country?

👋

When introducing yourself in another country — Al presentarte en otro país

✈️

During trips or at airports — En viajes o aeropuertos

📋

When filling out official forms — Al llenar formularios oficiales

💬

In conversations about culture — En conversaciones sobre cultura

 
02 / 07 ❓

Asking

How to ask about someone's country?

There are different ways depending on the level of formality.

😊

"¿De dónde eres?"

Informal - very common

👔

"¿De dónde es usted?"

Formal

 
03 / 07 💬

Responding

How to say your country?

THREE MAIN WAYS

"Soy de" + country/city

"Soy" + nationality

"Vengo de" + country/city

 
04 / 07 🗣️

Dialogues

Practical examples

INFORMAL

Sofía: "¿De dónde eres?"
Lucas: "Soy de Argentina. ¿Y tú?"
Sofía: "Yo soy colombiana."

FORMAL

Oficial: "¿De dónde es usted?"
Viajero: "Soy de España."

06 / 07 💭

Expressions

Talking about your country

"Vivo aquí desde hace 3 años" — I've been living here for 3 years

"Tengo doble nacionalidad" — I have dual nationality

"Nací en Italia pero crecí en Francia" — I was born in Italy but grew up in France

"Soy de México pero vivo en España" — I'm from Mexico but I live in Spain
