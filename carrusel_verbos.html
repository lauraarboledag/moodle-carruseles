Essential verb

The verb SER

The verb SER is one of the most important verbs in Spanish. We use it for:

🎯 IDENTITY

Say who you are: "Yo soy Ana"

🌍 ORIGIN

Say where you're from: "Yo soy de Colombia"

💼 PROFESSION

Say your job: "Yo soy profesor"

🎨 CHARACTERISTICS

Describe yourself: "Yo soy alto"

⚠️

Remember: The verb SER is irregular, so it changes a lot in each person

Reflexive verb

The verb LLAMARSE

The verb LLAMARSE is specifically used to say your name. It's a reflexive verb.

⚡ WHAT DOES "REFLEXIVE" MEAN?

A reflexive verb means the action "returns" to the person doing it. That's why we say "ME llamo" (I give myself a name)

BASIC STRUCTURE:

Pronoun + ME/TE/SE + LLAMO/LLAMAS...

EXAMPLES:

• Yo me llamo Ana

• Tú te llamas Carlos

• Él se llama Pedro

07 / 08 ⚖️

Comparison

SER vs LLAMARSE

Both verbs relate to identity, but they are used in different situations.

VERB SER

Soy Ana

To identify yourself

VERB LLAMARSE

Me llamo Ana

To say your name

MORE EXAMPLES

✓ Yo soy estudiante

(profession/occupation)

✓ Yo me llamo María

(proper name)

✓ Él es de Colombia

(origin/nationality)

✓ Ella se llama Laura

(her name)
