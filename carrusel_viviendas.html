Independent housing

The house

A casa (house) is an independent dwelling that does not share walls with other homes. It generally has its own land.

✓ CHARACTERISTICS

STRUCTURE:

Separate building with its own walls that do not touch other houses

LAND:

Has its own garden, yard or private outdoor space

LEVELS:

Can have one or several floors (ground floor, first floor, second floor...)

PRIVACY:

More privacy — you don't share walls with neighbors

COMMON TYPES

One-story house

All on one level

Two-story house

With a second floor

Country house

In a rural area

Urban house

In the city

02 / 10 🏢

Building dwelling

The apartment

An apartamento (also called piso or departamento) is a dwelling inside a building with many other homes.

🌎 NAMES BY COUNTRY

APARTAMENTO: Colombia, Venezuela, some Central American countries

DEPARTAMENTO: Mexico, Argentina, Chile, Peru

PISO: Spain

They are the same thing! Only the name changes.

✓ CHARACTERISTICS

LOCATION:

Inside a building with multiple floors and multiple apartments

SHARED WALLS:

You share walls with neighbors on the sides, above and/or below

COMMON AREAS:

Stairs, elevator, lobby, parking are shared

IDENTIFICATION:

Identified by number: Apartment 301, Floor 5B, Apt. 12

03 / 10 🏠

Open space

The studio or loft

A estudio or loft is a small apartment where the living room, dining room and bedroom are in the same open space, without walls separating them.

WHAT MAKES IT SPECIAL?

OPEN CONCEPT:

Everything is in ONE LARGE SPACE without divisions:
• The bed is in the same place as the living room
• The kitchen is open (integrated)
• There is no separate bedroom

ONLY PRIVATE ROOM:

The only closed room is the bathroom. Everything else is one big shared space.

✓ CHARACTERISTICS

Small size

20-50 m² typically

Open spaces

No internal divisions

For 1-2 people

Ideal for singles

More affordable

Lower cost than an apartment

04 / 10 🏘️

Attached houses

The townhouse

A casa adosada (also called casa en hilera or townhouse) are houses built attached to each other, sharing one or two side walls.

WHAT DOES A TOWNHOUSE LOOK LIKE?

PICTURE IT LIKE THIS:

🏠 | 🏠 | 🏠 | 🏠 | 🏠
↑
Several houses built next to each other,
sharing the side walls

UNIQUE CHARACTERISTICS:

• Each house has its own independent entrance
• Shares 1 or 2 walls with neighboring houses
• The front and back walls are private
• Usually 2-3 stories high
• Small private patio or garden (front or back)

DIFFERENCE FROM A NORMAL HOUSE

NORMAL HOUSE

Completely separate from other houses

TOWNHOUSE

Shares side walls with neighbors

It's like a middle ground between a house and an apartment

05 / 10 🏢

Two levels

The duplex

A dúplex is an apartment or dwelling that has two floors connected by an internal staircase. It's like having two apartments one on top of the other, but they are a single home.

MEANING OF "DÚPLEX"

DÚ = TWO
PLEX = LEVELS

→ TWO LEVELS in one dwelling

HOW DOES A DUPLEX WORK?

TYPICAL DISTRIBUTION:

FIRST FLOOR (DOWNSTAIRS):

Living room, dining room, kitchen, sometimes a guest bathroom

⬍ INTERNAL STAIRCASE ⬍

SECOND FLOOR (UPSTAIRS):

Bedrooms and bathrooms (private area)

IMPORTANT:

The staircase is INSIDE your home. It's not a shared building staircase, it's private just for you.

✓ ADVANTAGES

• More space than a regular apartment

• Separation between social and private areas

• Feeling of living in a small house

06 / 10 🏰

Luxury apartment

The penthouse

A penthouse (also called ático) is a luxury apartment located on the top floor of a building, usually with a private terrace and privileged views.

WHAT MAKES IT SPECIAL?

PRIVILEGED LOCATION:

It's on the highest floor of the building (top floor)

PRIVATE TERRACE:

Has a very large terrace or balcony, sometimes with a garden, pool or BBQ area

SPECTACULAR VIEWS:

360° panoramic view of the city — no buildings above to block

LARGER SIZE:

Generally much larger than other apartments in the building

LUXURY FINISHES:

Premium materials, higher ceilings, exclusive design

💰 PRICE

It's the most expensive type of housing in a building.
Associated with luxury, exclusivity and high status.

07 / 10 🌿

Rural / vacation home

The estate / chalet

A finca (Latin America) or chalet (Spain) is a house located in a rural or peri-urban area, with large land, nature and space for outdoor activities.

🌎 NAMES BY COUNTRY

FINCA: Colombia, Venezuela, Central America — can be recreational or agricultural

CHALET: Spain — single-family house with garden in residential area

RANCHO / HACIENDA: Mexico — large rural property

✓ CHARACTERISTICS

LARGE LAND:

Large green areas, gardens, orchards, fruit trees

FACILITIES:

Pool, BBQ, sports courts, stables, sheds depending on size

LOCATION:

Outside the city — countryside, mountains, coastal area

USE:

Vacations, weekend getaways or permanent residence

08 / 10 🎓

Shared housing

The student residence

A residencia estudiantil (also called dormitorio universitario or colegio mayor) is a building where university students live, usually near campus.

✓ CHARACTERISTICS

ROOM:

Private or shared room with another student(s)

COMMON AREAS:

Bathrooms, kitchen, study room, laundry — all shared

SERVICES:

Dining hall, wifi, security included in most cases

COST:

More affordable than an apartment — ideal for students
