Social area

The living room

The sala (also called salón or sala de estar) is the main space in the home for relaxing and receiving guests.

🌎 NAMES BY COUNTRY

SALA / SALA DE ESTAR: Colombia, Venezuela, Mexico

SALÓN: Spain

LIVING: Argentina, Chile, Uruguay

Dining area

The dining room

The comedor is the space intended for eating with family or friends. In small houses it can be integrated with the kitchen or living room.

06 / 11 🌅

Outdoor space

The balcony and terrace

The balcón and terraza are outdoor spaces connected to the home. They are ideal for enjoying the outdoors without leaving the house.

WHAT'S THE DIFFERENCE?

🏙️ BALCONY

Small, elevated, with railing. Typical in apartments.

☀️ TERRACE

Larger, can have furniture and plants. Sometimes on the roof.

OTHER OUTDOOR SPACES:

• El patio — open space on the ground floor
• El jardín — with plants and vegetation
• La azotea — terrace on the top of the building

💬 USEFUL PHRASES

• "Me gusta tomar café en el balcón."

• "La terraza tiene una vista preciosa."

07 / 11 🚪

Transition areas

The hallway and stairs

The pasillo and escaleras are the circulation and connection areas between the different spaces of the home.

CIRCULATION AREAS

EL PASILLO / CORREDOR:

Long, narrow space that connects rooms. Also called corredor in some countries.

LAS ESCALERAS:

Connect the different floors or levels of the home. Each step is called peldaño.

EL RECIBIDOR / LA ENTRADA:

The first space when entering the home. Also called hall or vestíbulo.

🔑 EXTRA VOCABULARY

la puerta

door

la ventana

window

el pasamanos

handrail / banister

el peldaño

step / stair

08 / 11 🚗

Service areas

The garage and basement

The garaje and sótano are practical and storage spaces. They are not always present in all homes.

WHAT ARE THEY?

EL GARAJE / PARQUEADERO:

Space to park the car. Garaje (Spain/Colombia), cochera (Mexico/Argentina), parqueadero (Colombia colloquial).

EL SÓTANO:

Underground level of the home. Used for storage or as an extra room.

EL CUARTO DE LAVADO / LAVANDERÍA:

Space with washing machine and dryer for laundry. Very common in Latin American homes.

🔧 USEFUL VOCABULARY

la lavadora

washing machine

la secadora

dryer

el almacenamiento

storage

el depósito

Grammar in context

Prepositions of place

To describe where objects or rooms are located, we use prepositions of place. They are essential for talking about your house.

📌 KEY PREPOSITIONS

encima de

on top of / above

debajo de

under / below

al lado de

next to / beside

entre

between

frente a

in front of / facing

detrás de

behind

💬 EXAMPLES AT HOME

• "El baño está al lado de la habitación."

• "La cocina está entre el comedor y el pasillo."

• "El televisor está frente al sofá."

• "Los zapatos están debajo de la cama."
