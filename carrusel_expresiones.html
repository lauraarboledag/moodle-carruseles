02 / 12  ·  👋 GREETINGS 👋

First impression

Formal and informal greetings

In shops and cafés, greetings are used according to the context. Here are the most common ones.

Buenos días

Good morning (until 12:00)

Buenas tardes

Good afternoon (12:00-20:00)

Buenas noches

Good evening / night (after 20:00)

Hola, ¿qué tal?

Hi, how are you? (informal)

¿Cómo está?

How are you? (formal, using "usted")

💬 EXAMPLE

"Buenos días, ¿cómo está?" – "Muy bien, gracias, ¿y usted?"

Magic words

Please, thank you, you're welcome

Using these words shows respect and good manners. They are essential in any interaction.

Por favor

please

Gracias / Muchas gracias

thank you / thank you very much

04 / 12  ·  🚪 PERMISSION 🚪

Asking politely

¿Puedo? / ¿Me permite?

To ask for something or request permission, use these expressions.

¿Puedo...? (informal)

Can I...?

¿Puede...? (formal)

Can you...?

¿Me permite...?

May I...? / Would you allow me...?

¿Le importa si...?

Do you mind if...?

💬 EXAMPLES

"¿Puedo probarme esta camisa?"
"¿Me permite pasar?"

Saying sorry

To get someone's attention, apologize, or say sorry for a mistake.

Disculpe / Disculpa (formal/informal)

Perdón / Perdone

06 / 12  ·  🗣️ TÚ / USTED 🗣️

Formality in Spanish

When to use "tú" and "usted"?

In shops and cafés, usted is often used with the assistant or waiter, although it varies by country and level of trust.

Tú (informal)

with friends, young people

Usted (formal)

with elders, strangers, in shops/restaurants

Verbs: "¿Tú quieres?" vs "¿Usted quiere?"

"Quieres" (tú) / "Quiere" (usted)

💡 NOTE

In Spain, "tú" is used more in informal settings. In Latin America, "usted" is very common in shops and cafés.

09 / 12  ·  🌎 CULTURE 🌎

Customs and differences

Politeness in different countries

Politeness can vary by region. Learn some specific characteristics.

Spain: "tú" very common, "por favor" and "gracias" always.

In cafés, "¿me pones un café?" is often used (informal but cordial).

Mexico and Colombia: frequent use of "usted" in shops, even among young people.

"Provecho" is said when you see someone eating.

Argentina: "vos" instead of "tú", but with the same cordial treatment.

"Disculpá" (informal) or "disculpe" (formal).

11 / 12  ·  🚨 ERRORS 🚨

Watch out!

Common politeness mistakes

❌ "Dame un café." (without please, too direct)

✅ "¿Me das un café, por favor?" or "¿Me podría dar un café?"

❌ Using "tú" with an older person in a shop (in countries where "usted" is expected)

✅ "¿Usted me puede ayudar?"

❌ "Gracias" without responding when someone thanks you

✅ Always reply with "de nada" or "no hay de qué".

❌ "¿Qué?" (can sound abrupt)

✅ "¿Cómo?" or "¿Perdón?" if you didn't understand.
