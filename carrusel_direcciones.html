Verbs for directions

When giving directions, we use verbs in the imperative (the mode for giving polite commands) or constructions with infinitives.

🔑 KEY VERBS

seguir / continuar

to continue / to keep going

➡️

girar / doblar / torcer

to turn

↪️

cruzar

to cross

🚦

subir / bajar

to go up / to go down

⬆️⬇️

llegar (a)

to arrive (at) / to reach

🏁

quedar (a / en)

to be located / to be situated

📍

Basic directions

Left, right and straight

The three most used directions when navigating are izquierda, derecha and recto/derecho. They are the basis of any direction.

⬅️

a la izquierda

to the left

⬆️

recto / derecho

straight ahead

➡️

a la derecha

to the right

🔄 TURN AROUND

Gira / Dobla a la izquierda

Turn left

Gira / Dobla a la derecha

Turn right

Sigue / Continúa recto

Keep going straight

Da la vuelta / Regresa

Turn around / Go back

💬 EXAMPLE

"Sigue recto dos cuadras, luego gira a la derecha y el banco está a la izquierda."

06 / 11 🙋

Real communication

How to ask for directions?

Before asking for directions, it's important to make a polite introduction. In Spanish, you always greet before asking for help.

💬 PHRASES TO ASK FOR DIRECTIONS

🌟 INTRODUCTION (always first)

"Disculpe / Perdone..."

"Buenas tardes, ¿me puede ayudar?"

DIRECT QUESTION:

"¿Dónde está / queda el banco?"

"Where is the bank?"

QUESTION WITH "SABER":

"¿Sabe usted cómo llegar al hospital?"

"Do you know how to get to the hospital?"

QUESTION OF DISTANCE:

"¿Está lejos de aquí la estación del metro?"

"Is the metro station far from here?"

ASK TO REPEAT:

"¿Puede repetir más despacio, por favor?"

"Can you repeat more slowly, please?"

💡 CULTURAL TIP

In Spanish-speaking countries, it's very common for people to stop and help with directions. Don't be afraid to ask!

07 / 11 🗣️

Real communication

How to give directions?

When giving directions, we use sequence connectors to order the steps, and visual references to better guide the person.

🔢 SEQUENCE CONNECTORS

Primero...

First...

Luego / Después...

Then / After that...

Al llegar a...

When you reach...

Por último / Al final...

Finally / At the end...

🗺️ COMPLETE EXAMPLE DIRECTIONS

"Primero, siga recto por esta calle dos cuadras.
Luego, gire a la derecha en la avenida principal.
Al llegar al semáforo, cruce la calle.
Por último, el hotel queda justo enfrente del parque, a la izquierda."

💡 USEFUL VISUAL REFERENCES

"...donde hay un semáforo" · "...frente al árbol grande"
"...a mano izquierda" · "...no tiene pérdida"
