Introduction

How do we use goodbyes?

We use goodbyes to close a conversation politely.

In Spanish, saying goodbye well is as important as greeting well.

When do we say goodbye?

🎒

We finish a class — Finalizamos una clase

🚪

We leave a work meeting — Salimos de una reunión de trabajo

📱

We finish a call or video call — Finalizamos una llamada o videollamada

🏛️

We leave a place — Nos vamos de un lugar

02 / 07  ·  🔀 CONSIDERATION 🔀

Consideration

It's important to know:

Goodbyes sometimes indicate whether we will see that person again or if there will be a future meeting.

🤝

"Hasta luego": We'll probably see that person again

👋

"Adiós": We might not see that person for a while

📅

"Hasta mañana/pronto": Indicates when we'll see them again

04 / 07  ·  💭 FUN FACT 💭

Fun fact

Hasta la vista, baby

💡

This phrase became very famous thanks to the movie Terminator, but it's actually not very common in everyday Spanish.

It's more of a fun expression we use when we want to sound dramatic or make a joke.

🎬 DID YOU KNOW...?

The original phrase in Spanish would be "Hasta la vista", which literally means "until we see each other again" — a goodbye that implies a future reunion.

05 / 07  ·  👔 FORMAL 👔

Formal goodbyes

Words and characteristics

FORMAL WORDS

Hasta luego Que tenga buen día Fue un placer Muchas gracias

CHARACTERISTICS

✓

More respectful tone

✓

Uses the formal "Usted"

✓

Clearer and slower pronunciation

✓

Maintain appropriate physical distance

06 / 07  ·  ✨ EXAMPLES ✨

Examples

Goodbyes in context

😊 INFORMAL

👋

"¡Chao! Nos vemos mañana en clase."

Context: between friends or classmates

👔 FORMAL

🤝

"Muchas gracias por su tiempo. Que tenga buen día."

Context: work meeting, customer service

💡 TIP

You can always combine expressions: "Hasta luego, que le vaya bien" sounds very natural and polite in any context.
