el pollo 🍗

fish

04 / 12  ·  🕗 MEALS 🕗

Eating routine

Breakfast, lunch, dinner

In Spanish, the main meals have specific names and schedules that vary by country.

el desayuno 🥐

breakfast (7:00-9:00)

el almuerzo / la comida 🍲

lunch (12:00-15:00)

la merienda 🍪

afternoon snack (17:00-18:00)

la cena 🍽️

dinner (20:00-22:00)

📌 FACT

In Spain, lunch is usually at 2:00-3:00 PM, and dinner after 9:00 PM.

05 / 12  ·  🍽️ VERBS 🍽️

Everyday actions

Comer, beber, tomar…

These regular verbs in the present tense are essential for talking about your eating habits.

yo como

I eat

tú comes

you eat

él/ella come

he/she eats

nosotros comemos

we eat

vosotros coméis

you (pl.) eat

ellos comen

they eat

beber (to drink) → yo bebo, tú bebes...

tomar (to take / drink) → yo tomo, tú tomas...

💬 EXAMPLES

"Yo como una manzana."
"¿Tú bebes café?"

06 / 12  ·  🍕 HUNGER / THIRST 🍕

What do you feel like?

Tener hambre, tener sed

To express that you want to eat or drink, we use the verb tener + noun.

tener hambre

to be hungry

tener sed

to be thirsty

tener ganas de + infinitive

to feel like (doing something)

💬 EXAMPLES

"Tengo hambre, voy a comer."
"¿Tienes sed? Toma agua."
"Tenemos ganas de tomar un café."

07 / 12  ·  🍽️ RESTAURANT 🍽️

Useful phrases

How to order food and drink

Learn to order politely using the verb querer (quiero) or the more formal me gustaría.

Quiero una ensalada

I want a salad

Me gustaría un café con leche

I would like a coffee with milk

¿Me puede traer la cuenta?

Can you bring me the bill?

Para mí, el pollo asado

For me, the roast chicken

10 / 12  ·  ❤️ GUSTAR ❤️

Expressing preferences

Me gusta / Me encanta

To talk about your favorite foods, use the verb gustar. Remember the agreement: singular/plural.

Me gusta el chocolate

I like chocolate

Me gustan las fresas

I like strawberries

Me encanta la pizza

I love pizza

No me gusta el pescado

I don't like fish

💬 QUESTION

"¿Qué te gusta comer?"
"Me gusta la pasta y las verduras."

11 / 12  ·  🚨 ERRORS 🚨

Watch out!

Common mistakes with food

❌ "El agua está frío."

✅ "El agua está fría." (agua is feminine but uses "el")

❌ "Me gusta los tacos."

✅ "Me gustan los tacos."

❌ "Tengo hambre y sed."

✅ Correct, but remember: "tengo hambre" and "tengo sed".

❌ "Yo quiero una cerveza." (sounds abrupt)

✅ "Quisiera / Me gustaría una cerveza." (more polite)
