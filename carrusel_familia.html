Immediate family

The nuclear family

The nuclear family is the closest family: parents and children. They are the people you usually live with.

👨‍👩 Parents

Masculine

PADRE

or PAPÁ

Informal: papi, pa

Feminine

MADRE

or MAMÁ

Informal: mami, ma

Masculine

HIJO

Your son

Feminine

👫 Siblings

Feminine

Birth order

Older or younger?

In Spanish we specify if a sibling is older (born before you) or younger (born after you).

👴

OLDER SIBLING

Born BEFORE you

EXAMPLE:

Yo tengo 20 años, mi hermano tiene 25 → Él es mi hermano mayor

👶

YOUNGER SIBLING

Born AFTER you

EXAMPLE:

Yo tengo 20 años, mi hermana tiene 15 → Ella es mi hermana menor

Second generation

Grandparents and grandchildren

Grandparents are your parents' parents. Grandchildren are your children's children.

👴👵 Grandparents — your parents' parents

👴

ABUELO

Your dad's or mom's father

👵

ABUELA

Your dad's or mom's mother

PLURAL:

Los abuelos → when you talk about both together

👶 Grandchildren — your children's children

👦

NIETO

Your son's or daughter's son

👧

NIETA

Your son's or daughter's daughter

Extended family

Uncles and nephews

Uncles are your parents' siblings. Nephews are your siblings' children.

👨‍👩 Uncles — your parents' siblings

Masculine

TÍO

Your dad's or mom's brother

Feminine

TÍA

Your dad's or mom's sister

EXAMPLE:

Mi papá tiene un hermano: Juan → Juan es mi tío.
Mi mamá tiene una hermana: Ana → Ana es mi tía.

👶 Nephews — your siblings' children

Masculine

SOBRINO

Your brother's/sister's son

Feminine

SOBRINA

Your brother's/sister's daughter

EXAMPLE:

Mi hermana tiene un hijo: Pedro → Pedro es mi sobrino.
Mi hermano tiene una hija: Lucía → Lucía es mi sobrina.

Same generation

Cousins

Cousins are your uncles' children. They are like siblings, but from a "next door" generation.

Who are they?

Masculine

PRIMO

Your uncle's/aunt's son

Feminine

PRIMA

Your uncle's/aunt's daughter

Family diagram

TÍO José + TÍA Laura
↓
Their son Carlos = My PRIMO
Their daughter María = My PRIMA

Complete example

Mi papá tiene un hermano: José
José and his wife have two children: Carlos and María

→ Carlos is my primo
→ María is my prima

Husband and partner

Feminine

NOVIA

In-law family

In-laws, son/daughter-in-law

In-laws are your spouse's family members. They are created when you get married.

👴👵 In-laws — your spouse's parents

Masculine

SUEGRO

Your spouse's father

Feminine

SUEGRA

Your spouse's mother

Me casé con Ana → Ana's parents are my suegros.

👨👰 Son/daughter-in-law — your children's spouses

Masculine

YERNO

Your daughter's husband

Feminine

NUERA

Your son's wife

Mi hija se casó con Pedro → Pedro es mi yerno.
Mi hijo se casó con Laura → Laura es mi nuera.

08 / 10  ·  👫 👫

Siblings-in-law

Brothers/sisters-in-law

In-laws are your spouse's siblings, or the spouses of your siblings.

There are two types:

TYPE 1:

Your spouse's siblings

TYPE 2:

Your siblings' spouses

Example type 1

Me casé con María.
María has a brother: Juan → Juan is my cuñado.
María has a sister: Ana → Ana is my cuñada.

Example type 2

Mi hermana se casó con Pedro → Pedro es mi cuñado.
Mi hermano se casó con Laura → Laura es mi cuñada.

Useful expressions

How to introduce your family

The most common phrases to talk about your family in Spanish.

Using "MI" (singular)

• Mi padre se llama Juan
• Mi madre es profesora
• Mi hermano tiene 15 años
• Mi esposa es de México

Using "MIS" (plural)

• Mis padres viven en Colombia
• Mis hermanos son mayores que yo
• Mis abuelos tienen 80 años
• Mis tíos viven en España

To introduce someone

• Te presento a mi esposo, Carlos
• Él es mi hermano mayor
• Ella es mi prima, Ana
• Ellos son mis padres
