Welcome to the topic

What is a piece of furniture?

A mueble is a movable object used to furnish (decorate and equip) the spaces of a home. Furniture makes a house comfortable and functional.

💡 VOCABULARY FACT

The word "mueble" comes from Latin mobilis, which means "that can be moved".
That's why we also say inmueble for a house — because it doesn't move!

02 / 12 🛋️

Social area

Living room furniture

The living room is the most social space in the house. Its furniture should be comfortable for relaxing, chatting and receiving guests.

🪑 MAIN FURNITURE

el sofá

sofa / couch

Long upholstered seat. Also called canapé (Spain) or sofá de 2/3 puestos depending on its size.

el sillón

armchair

Comfortable chair with armrests for one person. Larger than a regular chair.

la mesa de centro

coffee table

Low table placed in front of the sofa. Used for drinks, magazines, and remote controls.

el estante / la repisa

bookshelf / shelf

Furniture with multiple levels for books, decorations, and decorative items.

el mueble de TV

TV stand / entertainment center

Surface or low furniture where the TV is placed. Also called rack.

💬 EXAMPLE

"El sofá está frente al mueble de TV y la mesa de centro está en el medio."

04 / 12 🍽️

Dining area

Dining room furniture

Dining room furniture is designed for eating, gathering and sharing. Its size depends on available space and the number of people.

🪑 MAIN FURNITURE

la mesa de comedor

dining table

The centerpiece of the dining room. Can be round, rectangular, or square. We talk about tables for 4, 6, or 8 people.

las sillas de comedor

dining chairs

Placed around the table. They should be comfortable but not as cushioned as living room chairs.

el aparador / la vitrina

sideboard / china cabinet

Furniture against the wall for storing dishes, glassware, and tablecloths. Vitrina has glass doors for display.

la silla alta / la trona

high chair / bar stool

Elevated chair for small children or kitchen counters. Trona is specifically for babies.

💬 EXAMPLE

"Tenemos una mesa de seis puestos y un aparador con la vajilla de la abuela."

05 / 12 🛏️

Rest area

Bedroom furniture

Bedroom furniture should promote rest and organization. The bed is the central element around which everything else is arranged.

🛏️ MAIN FURNITURE

la cama

bed

The main piece. Sizes: sencilla (twin), doble (full/double), queen, king. Includes headboard, bed frame, and mattress.

el armario / el clóset

wardrobe / closet

For storing clothes. Armario is a standalone piece; clóset is built into the wall.

la mesita de noche

nightstand / bedside table

Small table next to the bed. Used for a cell phone, water glass, or book. Also called velador.

la cómoda / el gavetero

dresser / chest of drawers

Furniture with drawers for storing underwear, socks, and folded clothes.

💬 EXAMPLE

"Mi habitación tiene una cama doble, dos mesitas de noche y un clóset empotrado."

07 / 12 🍳

Preparation area

Kitchen furniture

Kitchen furniture is designed for storing, organizing, and facilitating food preparation. They are functional and practical.

🪑 MAIN FURNITURE

los gabinetes / armarios de cocina

kitchen cabinets

Drawers and doors built into the wall for storing utensils, dishes, and food. The lower ones are called muebles bajos and the upper ones alacenas.

la alacena / la despensa

pantry / food cabinet

Tall cabinet for storing non-perishable food: rice, canned goods, oil, spices.

la encimera / el mesón

countertop / kitchen counter

The horizontal surface where food is prepared. Encimera in Spain, mesón in Colombia and Venezuela.

la isla de cocina

kitchen island

A central freestanding piece with a work surface. Allows you to cook in the center of the kitchen.

💬 EXAMPLE

"Los platos están en la alacena, encima del mesón."

08 / 12 💻

Study area

Study furniture

The estudio or despacho is the space for working, studying, and concentrating. Its furniture combines functionality with comfort.

🪑 MAIN FURNITURE

el escritorio

desk

Large, sturdy table for working on a computer, writing, or studying. May have drawers on the sides.

la silla de escritorio

office chair

Ergonomic chair with wheels and adjustable height. Designed for spending several hours sitting.

la biblioteca / el librero

bookcase / bookshelf

Tall furniture with shelves for organizing books. Librero in Mexico, biblioteca in Colombia.

el archivador

filing cabinet

Metal or wooden furniture with drawers for storing documents and organized folders.

💬 EXAMPLE

"El escritorio está frente a la ventana y al lado hay una biblioteca llena de libros."

09 / 12 🚿

Hygiene area

Bathroom furniture

The bathroom has specific furniture for organizing hygiene products and making practical use of the limited space.

🪑 MAIN FURNITURE

el mueble de baño / el vanity

bathroom vanity

Low furniture on which the sink is installed. Has drawers or doors for toiletries.

el botiquín

medicine cabinet

Small cabinet (usually with a mirror) installed on the wall. Stores medications and hygiene products.

el toallero

towel rack / towel bar

Bar or structure mounted on the wall for hanging towels. Can be wall-mounted or freestanding.

la estantería / los estantes

shelves / shelving unit

Shelf or rack mounted on the wall for organizing shampoos, creams, and hygiene items.

💬 EXAMPLE

"El botiquín está encima del lavamanos y los toalleros están al lado de la ducha."

Describing furniture

To describe a piece of furniture, we use adjectives of material, size, shape, and color. These adjectives agree in gender and number with the furniture.

🪵 MATERIALS

📐 SIZE AND SHAPE

grande / pequeño

redondo / cuadrado
