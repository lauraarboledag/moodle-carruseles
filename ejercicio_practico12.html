Grammatical note

The verbs gustar and encantar are conjugated according to what is liked (the subject). We use gusta / encanta with a singular noun or infinitive, and gustan / encantan with a plural noun. They are always accompanied by an indirect object pronoun (me, te, le, nos, os, les).
