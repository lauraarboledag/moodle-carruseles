Essential grammar

Reflexive verbs

Reflexive verbs describe actions that one does to oneself. They are recognized because in the infinitive they end in -se: levantarse, ducharse, peinarse…

03 / 12  ·  🌅 MORNING 🌅

Daily activities — part 1

Morning routine

Morning activities are the first of the day. Most are reflexive verbs because we do them to ourselves.

⏰ MORNING SEQUENCE

1️⃣

despertarse

to wake up · "Me despierto a las 6."

2️⃣

levantarse

to get up · "Me levanto y abro las ventanas."

3️⃣

ducharse / bañarse

to shower / to bathe · "Me ducho con agua caliente."

4️⃣

cepillarse los dientes

to brush teeth · "Me cepillo los dientes."

5️⃣

vestirse / arreglarse

to get dressed / to get ready

6️⃣

desayunar

to have breakfast · "Desayuno arepas y café."

💬 COMPLETE EXAMPLE

"Me despierto a las seis, me ducho, me visto y desayuno antes de salir."

04 / 12  ·  🧴 PERSONAL CARE 🧴

Vocabulary — Personal care

Personal care verbs

These verbs describe body care. Almost all are reflexive because the subject does the action on themselves. Note that many go with an article, not a possessive.

🪥 PERSONAL CARE VERBS

lavarse la cara

to wash one's face

lavarse las manos

to wash one's hands

peinarse

to comb one's hair

maquillarse

to put on makeup

afeitarse

to shave

ponerse la ropa

to put on clothes

secarse el pelo

to dry one's hair

pintarse las uñas

to paint one's nails

⚠️ ARTICLE, NOT POSSESSIVE

Say "me lavo la cara" (not "mi cara")
because the reflexive already indicates it's yours.

06 / 12  ·  🌙 NIGHT 🌙

Daily activities — part 3

Night routine

The night mixes rest, leisure and preparation for sleep. Reflexive verbs appear again at the end of the day.

🌃 NIGHT SEQUENCE

🏠

volver a casa / llegar a casa

to come back home / to arrive home

🍜

cenar / preparar la cena

to have dinner / to prepare dinner

📺

ver la televisión / una serie

to watch TV / a series

📖

leer un libro / revisar el teléfono

to read / to check the phone

😴

acostarse / dormirse

to go to bed / to fall asleep

💬 EXAMPLE

"Llego a casa, ceno algo ligero, veo una serie y me acuesto a las diez."

11 / 12  ·  🚨 MISTAKES 🚨

Pay attention!

Common mistakes with reflexives

❌ MISTAKE 1 — Omitting the reflexive pronoun

"Yo levanto a las siete."

✅ "Yo me levanto a las siete."

❌ MISTAKE 2 — Wrong pronoun

"Ella se levanto temprano."

✅ "Ella se levanta temprano." (present, not past)

❌ MISTAKE 3 — Possessive instead of article

"Me lavo mis manos."

✅ "Me lavo las manos." (article, not possessive)

❌ MISTAKE 4 — Incomplete double negation

"Nunca desayuno no."

✅ "Nunca desayuno." / "No desayuno nunca."
